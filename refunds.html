Refund Policy

Effective September 12, 2026 · Last updated October 5, 2026

Changed October 5, 2026: the founding year no longer renews on its own; a plan paid by invoice never does; how to cancel a subscription now names Plan and billing. None of these adds a charge.

Nothing is charged today. The tester program is free — no card, nothing to refund. From 11 January 2027, ask us for an access code. A code gives your account a 30-day free trial with no card up front, and nothing is charged during it either. A trial does not turn into a paid subscription on its own.

This is the policy that applies once you choose a paid plan and pay for it. It is part of the Terms of Service.

Who you are buying from

Weeksolved, a California sole proprietorship, is the seller. You buy from us, not from a reseller. Stripe processes the payment and runs the billing portal; we never see or store your full card number. Because we are the seller, a refund is our decision and not somebody else's policy — which is why this page is short.

30-day guarantee on your first payment

If a paid plan isn't right for you, tell us within 30 days of your first payment and we'll refund it in full — monthly or annual, no hoops, no questions about why. One request by email is all it takes.

Canceling

If you pay by invoice there is nothing to cancel: the term ends on its date and nothing further is charged. If you want to stop before then, ask and we will refund the unused days pro-rata. You can also cancel by emailing us from the address on the account.

Canceling stops the next charge. Your paid features run to the end of the period you have already paid for. If you would rather stop now and have the unused days back, ask and we will refund them pro-rata to the card you paid with.

Your data stays exportable for 30 days after cancellation — export anything you want to keep inside that window.

Renewals, and the reminder before one

A subscription started at checkout renews automatically until you cancel it. A plan paid by invoice does not renew, so this section does not apply to it. Before every annual renewal we email the address on your account, between 45 and 15 days ahead, telling you what will be charged, when, and how to cancel.

If an annual renewal still catches you by surprise, tell us within 14 days of the charge and we'll refund it in full.

Adding locations

Adding a location mid-period? The unused time on what you are already paying for is credited pro-rata against the new amount, so you never pay twice for the same days.

The founding rate

Founding customers pay $290 per location for the first year. The 30-day guarantee applies to that payment in full.

The founding year is paid once, by invoice, and does not renew on its own. At least 30 days before it ends we send an invoice for the next year at the standard rate then in effect. If you do not pay it, nothing is charged, and your data stays exportable for 30 days.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back through Stripe to the original card, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank. We cannot send a refund to a different card or by another route — that is a rule of the card networks, not ours. You will get an email from us when we issue it.

If a charge looks wrong

Email us before you call your bank. A chargeback takes weeks and ends up in the same place: because we are the seller, the dispute comes to us and we answer it ourselves, from your account's records. A refund we agree takes minutes, and if we got it wrong we would rather fix it than argue about it.
