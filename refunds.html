Refund Policy

Effective August 31, 2026

Right now Weeksolved is free for everyone in the tester program — there is nothing to refund. This is the policy that applies once paid plans begin.

30-day guarantee

If a paid plan isn't right for you, tell us within 30 days of your first payment and we'll refund it in full — monthly or annual, no questions that matter, no hoops.

Cancelling

Cancel any time from your account. Cancellation takes effect immediately — access to paid features ends right away, and we refund the unused portion of whatever you'd paid, pro-rated to the day. Your data stays exportable after cancellation.

Upgrades

Moving up a tier? The unused time on your current plan is credited pro-rata against the new one, so you never pay twice for the same days.

Renewals

Annual renewals are emailed in advance. If a renewal catches you by surprise, contact us within 14 days of the charge and we'll refund it.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are issued by our payment provider to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.
