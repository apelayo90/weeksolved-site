What's included

  • Drafts the whole week from your rules — availability, requests off, guaranteed hours, roles and licences, coverage minimums, and work patterns
  • Meal, rest, overtime and double-time rule sets to pick from: California, New York, Washington, Oregon, Colorado, Illinois, Nevada and a federal baseline
  • Names the exact rule that stopped each person it could not place, then ranks the fixes — including multi-person swap chains and a one-press Fix everything you can
  • Flags any shift that would break your house cap, your staffing window, or daily overtime and double-time rules, and warns about major holidays even before you set up a holiday list
  • Never rewrites a week you have already posted
  • Wall sheet, per-person handouts, PDF, CSV/XLSX with hours and overtime split (holiday pay included), and calendar files
  • PTO balances, a fairness ledger, and labor cost with the overtime and double-time split
  • Keeps working when the connection drops. Setup is about ten minutes of questions

What it does not do

  • No time clock — it builds the week; it does not record who actually clocked in
  • No employee app — only the manager signs in
  • No messaging platform
  • No payroll-provider integration
  • Works out no pay for missed breaks: it flags them and leaves any extra pay to your payroll

Weeksolved is scheduling software, not legal advice — you stay in charge of your own compliance. Prices in USD, plus any tax your location requires.

Why flat beats per-employee

Most scheduling tools charge per staff member per month and need every employee to install an app and keep a login. Weeksolved turns that round: only the manager signs in, and staff get their schedule by text, email, a printout or a calendar file. So there is nothing to count.

Fair questions

Is it really free right now?

Yes. During the tester program everything is free, with no card and no clock beyond your access code's window. Testers who stay on are exactly who the founding rate above is for.

What happens when the trial opens at general release?

From 11 January 2027, ask us for an access code. A code gives your account 30 days free, no card required. If you decide not to continue, your data stays yours: spreadsheets and PDFs export while the trial runs, and after it ends the whole board downloads as one backup file.

Do my employees need accounts?

No. Only the manager signs in. Staff get their schedules the way they already live — text, email, a printout, or a calendar file that drops shifts straight into their phone. Nothing to install, nobody locked out, and nobody to pay for.

Are the break rules part of the price, or an upgrade?

Part of the price. There is one plan, and this is what it does: the state break rules you pick apply to every shift, on the free trial too. It was never going to be something we held back for more money.

How does the price change with more locations?

It doesn't. $470 a location a year ($39.17 a month, one invoice), at one location or ten. Add a location and you pay for one more location; nothing else moves.

What's the founding customer offer?

Five businesses, invited from the tester program, join at $290 a location for the first year, paid once by invoice, in exchange for a short reference once you're up and running. Nothing renews on its own. Before the year ends we send an invoice for the next one at the standard price then in effect, and you continue only if you pay it. It is offered by invitation and is not transferable.

What if it isn't right for us?

Your first payment is covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee. Outside of that, cancel any time — you keep access through the period you already paid for, and your data exports freely while your account is open and for 30 days after.

Do managers or admins count as paid users?

There are no paid users at all. The price is for the location, so it does not matter how many people manage the schedule or how many staff are on it.
