Why flat beats per-employee

Most scheduling tools charge $2.50 to $5 per staff member per month and need every employee to install an app and keep a login. Weeksolved turns that round: only the manager signs in, and staff get their schedule by text, email, a printout or a calendar file. So there is nothing to count.

Fair questions

Is it really free right now?

Yes. During the tester program everything is free, with no card and no clock beyond your access code's window. Testers who stay on get first claim on a founding price, locked for as long as they keep subscribing.

What happens when the trial ends later on?

When billing opens on 11 January 2027, every new account starts with 30 days free and every feature on, no card up front. If you decide not to continue, your data stays yours: schedules, team, everything goes out as a spreadsheet or PDF whenever you want.

Do my employees need accounts?

No. Only the manager signs in. Staff get their schedules the way they already live — text, email, a printout, or a calendar file that drops shifts straight into their phone. Nothing to install, nobody locked out, and nobody to pay for.

Is the break-law engine really on the cheapest plan?

Yes, and on the free trial. Your state's meal and rest break rules are applied to every shift on every plan. A missed meal in California costs an hour's pay per day per person; that is not something to hold back for a higher tier.

Then what is Pro for?

The complicated floor. Named posts that cover each other's meals, work patterns like seven-on-seven-off and hours averaged over a period, one fair draw for the season's vacations, PTO balances that accrue, licence and certification expiry, and the record of who got what. If your week is a roster and a set of hours, Solo is the right plan.

How does the multi-location price work?

Your first location pays its plan price. Every location after it pays half that plan's price, monthly or yearly, for as long as it runs. Five pharmacies on Pro are $49 + 4 × $24.50 = $147 a month, not $245. There is no minimum, no bundle to buy into, and nothing to configure: tell us which accounts belong together and the discount is set up by hand.

Can my people work across more than one location?

Yes. One roster across all your sites is here now: add each location under Settings, say which one every coverage row and post belongs to, and tick on each person's profile where they can work. Somebody who floats between two sites is drafted once, with their hours totalled across both, so overtime is right rather than counted twice. The week view, the day sheet and the reports all filter by location. Two things are still to come and neither costs extra: separate opening hours per site (the drafter currently works from one set of hours for the whole account), and a manager login limited to a single site — today one login sees them all.

Do managers or admins count as paid users?

There are no paid users at all. The price is for the location, so it does not matter how many people manage the schedule or how many staff are on it.
