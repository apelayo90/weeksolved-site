Privacy Policy

Effective August 31, 2026

This policy explains what Weeksolved stores and why, in plain language.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a password (held by our authentication provider — we never see the password itself).
  • Schedule data: what you enter to run your schedule — staff names, roles, availability, hours, and any contact details or notes you add. You control this data; we store it so it follows you between devices.
  • Problem reports and messages: what you write when reporting a problem or contacting us, plus technical context (screen, app version, device type). Reports never include your staff's names, hours, or contact details.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of your data with anyone for marketing.
  • No analytics trackers, no third-party cookies, no fingerprinting.
  • Nothing you type in the help assistant leaves your device.

Where data lives

Account and schedule data are stored with Google Cloud (Firebase) in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest, in a database whose access rules allow each account to read only its own data. A copy also lives in your own browser so the app works offline.

Payments

When paid plans begin, payments will be handled by a merchant of record; we never see or store card numbers or bank details.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. Email us and we will delete your account and its data; exports are available inside the app any time. Tester accounts that expire keep their data so a returning code can pick up where it left off, unless you ask us to remove it.

Your staff's information

If you enter your employees' information, you are the one who decides what goes in and who it's shared with; we process it only to provide the service to you. Employees can ask you, the account holder, to view or remove their information.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions or requests: weeksolved@gmail.com.
