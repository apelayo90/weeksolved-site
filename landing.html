It never invents a shift

Weeksolved schedules only the shifts you've defined. If your coverage can't be met from that pool, the slot stays open and it tells you the rule that stopped each person — then shows the best ways out, clearly labeled, least disruptive first.

It works from your break rules

Every drafted shift gets its meal placed to the state break rules you pick, rest breaks laid around it, and staggering that never drops coverage below your minimums. Late or missing meals it catches are flagged as you draft — look over the schedule before you post it.

No employee accounts. Ever.

Only the manager signs in. Staff get their schedules the way they already live — text, email, print, or a calendar file that drops shifts straight into their phone. Nothing to install, nothing to teach.

Fair, and you can see it

Sought-after shifts rotate by seniority, by turns, or by an open lottery. A ledger tracks who you've given what, so the same people don't draw the short straw week after week — and you can see it at a glance.

Built for licensed workplaces

Ratios between roles, license and certification expiry tracking, posts that must never sit empty with named meal-break relief. The depth enterprise scheduling suites keep for enterprise contracts, at a flat small-business price.

Your data stays yours

Schedules live in your private account and follow you between devices. No ads, no ad trackers, no selling your data — and everything exports to Excel and PDF whenever you want it. Your staff’s names, numbers and pay rates are never sold, rented, or handed to anyone for their own purposes; that one is written into the terms, not just promised here.

The break-rules engine

Meal and rest break rules differ by state.

Weeksolved carries the break rules for the states it supports: how long a shift runs before a meal is owed, the latest the rule allows it to start, how many paid rest breaks the hours earn, and when a waiver is allowed. Every draft applies them automatically, and every printed schedule shows where you placed each meal and rest break, so you can check the week before it goes up.
