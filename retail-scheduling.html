Scheduling for shops and retail

A shop week is an opening shift, a closing shift, a Saturday that needs everyone, and a December that needs people you have not hired yet.

Free during testing · $29 a month flat when billing opens

Weeksolved drafts a week for sales associates, shift leads, cashiers and stockroom staff from the rules you set, checks it against your state's meal and rest break law before it goes up, and tells you exactly which rule stopped a person when a shift cannot be filled. It costs one flat price for the location, and your staff never need an account.

Open, close, and the bit in the middle

Weeksolved drafts around the shape a shop actually runs: somebody opening before the door does, somebody closing after it, and the cover in between set by how busy each weekday is rather than by an even split. Tell it which days are heavy and the draft weights them.

Fair-workweek notice, if your city has it

Several cities require the schedule to be published a set number of days ahead, and pay a premium when it changes afterwards. Weeksolved tracks how far ahead you have posted, warns before a change would trigger a premium, and keeps the record of what was posted and when — which is the part people wish they had when somebody asks.

Seasonal hiring that doesn't change the bill

Take on eight people for the holidays and your price does not move. Per-user pricing punishes exactly the month you need the most people. When the season ends, set them aside rather than deleting them: off every schedule, everything kept, and one press puts them back next year.

Breaks placed properly, on the cheap plan

Meals inside their legal window, rest breaks laid around them, and staggering that never leaves the floor uncovered while somebody is on their fifteen. That is on the cheapest plan and the whole trial, because a schedule that breaks the law cheaply is not a bargain.

Nobody is locked out of their own schedule

Part-time retail staff are the group least likely to have installed the app. Weeksolved has one account — the manager's — and everybody else gets the schedule by text, email, print, or a calendar file. Nothing to install, nobody chasing a password reset on a Sunday morning.

The till count and the shutter

Opening and closing lists live on the day sheet, tick by tick, and fall to whoever actually opens and closes that day. When somebody swaps, the list moves with the schedule.

What it will not do

Weeksolved does not forecast staffing from your point-of-sale takings. It schedules to coverage rules you set. If you want the schedule driven by a sales forecast, another tool does that.

What it costs

One flat price per location — $29 a month on Solo, $49 on Pro, two months free paid yearly — whatever the headcount. No per-user fees, no paid admin seats, no charge for the manager who covers weekends. Break law is on every plan including the cheapest. During testing it is free with an access code.
