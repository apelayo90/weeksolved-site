Questions, answered plainly

Everything people ask before they choose Weeksolved. Anything missing? Ask us — the answer will probably end up on this page.

How does pricing work?

Flat, per location. Solo is $29 a month and Pro is $49, with two months free if you pay yearly. Your state's meal and rest break rules are applied on both, including Solo — that part is never held back for a higher plan. Never per employee either: a team of 8 and a team of 40 at one location pay exactly the same. Full details are on the pricing page.

Is there a free trial? Do I need a credit card?

Yes, and no card. When billing opens, every new account starts with 30 days free with every feature on. Scheduling runs on a weekly rhythm, so that's four full scheduling cycles to judge it. Right now, during the tester program, everything is free with an access code.

What happens when my trial ends?

You pick a plan, or you don't. If you don't continue, your data stays exportable — schedules, team, everything goes out as a spreadsheet (CSV) and PDF whenever you want. Nothing is held hostage.

Can I cancel anytime? Are there contracts or hidden fees?

Cancel any time, no contracts, no setup fees, no per-user fees. Canceling stops the renewal and your access runs to the end of the period you paid for; if you would rather stop now and have the unused days back, ask and we will refund them — the exact terms are in the refund policy.

Do managers or admins count as paid users?

There are no paid users at all. Pricing is per location, so it doesn't matter how many people manage the schedule or how many staff are on it.

What happens when I hire or someone leaves?

Nothing changes on your bill. Add or remove people freely — the price is the same on the day you hire your 30th employee as it was with your 8th. A departing employee's history stays in your schedule records.

Do my employees need accounts or an app?

No — that's the point. Only the manager signs in. Staff get their schedules the way they already live: text, email, a printout, or a calendar file that drops shifts straight into their phone. Nothing to install, nothing to teach, nobody locked out.

What does it run on?

Any modern browser — phone, tablet, or computer, Mac or Windows, iPhone or Android. There's nothing to install and everything you can do on a computer you can also do on a phone.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. On the sign-in screen, press Sign in with Google. If you already have a Weeksolved account under that email address it is the same account — your board is right where you left it. New to Weeksolved? After Google confirms who you are, you enter your access code and you are in. Not yet on an iPhone or iPad, where Google’s sign-in window cannot reliably hand the answer back to the page — there, use your email and password for now, and you can add a password to a Google account under Settings → Your account.

How long does setup take?

About ten minutes, once. The setup questions ask about your hours, roles, shifts, team, and rules — most answers are just picking an option, and team lists can be pasted in whole. After that, a week is one button.

How does the break-law compliance work?

Pick your state and every drafted shift gets its meal placed inside the legal window, rest breaks laid around it, and staggering that never drops coverage below your minimums. This is on every plan, including the cheapest and the free trial. Problems are flagged before the schedule goes up, and printed schedules show where every break was placed. Weeksolved is scheduling software, not legal advice — you stay in charge of your own compliance.

Will it ever schedule something I didn't ask for?

No. It only ever schedules shifts you've defined, from the pool you set. If your coverage can't be met, the slot stays open and it tells you exactly which rule stopped each person — then shows you the ways out, clearly labeled, least disruptive first.

What happens when coverage dips or a rule is broken?

Every flagged problem carries a Fix button — on Home, in the checks panel, on every coverage dip on the day sheet, and on the break sheet. It opens one short list: what fixes it this week (stretch a shift already on the day with the overtime priced, move a meal to a time the floor holds, waive a meal where the law allows, bring someone in, hand a shift to someone who is clear) and what stops it coming back (let a meal count as on duty for that rule, close the doors for lunch on solo days, take a day out of a rule, schedule one more person that weekday, change your hours). One option is marked Recommended. Nothing is invented — every option uses shifts, people and rules you already have — and the checks run again the moment you press one.

What counts as a “location”?

One place of business with its own hours, team, and coverage needs. If you run one pharmacy, you're one location. Three stores are three separate accounts — each with its own roster, hours and rules, kept apart on purpose so two sites can never overwrite each other's week. Running several? Talk to us.

Do you share my staff's names, contact details or pay?

No. Nothing that identifies one of your people — name, phone, email, hourly rate, accrued time off, license dates, your notes, or their schedule history — is sold, rented, published, given to anyone for their own purposes, or used to train an AI model. That is section 3.1 of the Terms, so it is a promise we owe you contractually, not a policy line we could quietly rewrite. There are three ways it can leave your account and none of them is us deciding to pass it on: you send it, our database provider stores it so the service can exist, or valid legal process compels it — and then we tell you first unless we are legally forbidden from doing so. Worth knowing too: none of it is required. Weeksolved drafts a full week from first names alone.

Is my team's data private and secure?

Your schedules live in your private account, encrypted in transit and at rest on Google Cloud infrastructure, and no one else's account can see them. There are no ads, no trackers, and your data is never sold. The security page spells it all out.

What payment methods will you accept?

Checkout is handled by our payment partner and takes the usual cards and wallets, with prices in USD plus any tax your location requires — collected and remitted by the payment partner, so tax is never a surprise line on your books.

What if I get stuck or find a problem?

The Help & FAQ page inside the app answers most things in seconds, the ⚑ Report button files a problem in ten, and Contact us messages go straight to a person. A real human reads every message and replies to the email on your account.
