It never invents a shift

Weeksolved schedules only the shifts you've defined. If your coverage can't be met from that pool, the slot stays open and it tells you exactly which limit stopped each person — then shows the best ways out, clearly labeled, least disruptive first.

It knows your break law

Pick your state and every drafted shift gets its meal placed inside the legal window, rest breaks laid around it, and staggering that never drops coverage below your minimums. Late or missing meals are flagged before the schedule goes up — not after a claim lands.

No employee accounts. Ever.

Only the manager signs in. Staff get their schedules the way they already live — text, email, print, or a calendar file that drops shifts straight into their phone. Nothing to install, nothing to teach.

Fair, and it can prove it

Sought-after shifts rotate by seniority, by turns, or by an auditable lottery. A ledger tracks who's had what, so the same people don't draw the short straw week after week — and you can show the receipts.

Built for licensed workplaces

Your data stays yours

Schedules live in your private account and follow you between devices. No ads, no trackers, no selling your data — and everything exports to Excel and PDF whenever you want it.

The break-law engine

Meal and rest break rules differ by state, and the penalties are real — in California a single missed or late meal period owes an hour of premium pay, per person, per day.

Weeksolved carries each state's rules: how long a shift runs before a meal is owed, the latest legal moment it can start, how many paid rest breaks the hours earn, and when a waiver is allowed. Every draft applies them automatically, and every printed schedule carries the paper trail that shows the breaks were placed lawfully — the first thing anyone asks for.

Weeksolved is scheduling software, not legal advice — you stay in charge of your own compliance.
