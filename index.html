Why managers switch

The schedule does what you'd do — just in seconds

Most scheduling apps hand you an empty grid and wish you luck. Weeksolved asks what your week has to look like, then builds it — and shows its work.

It never invents a shift

Only the shifts you've defined ever get scheduled. If coverage can't be met from that pool, the slot stays open and it names the rule that stopped each person — then offers the best ways out, clearly labeled, least disruptive first.

It works from your break rules

Every drafted shift gets its meal placed to the state break rules you pick, rest breaks laid around it, and staggering that never drops the floor below your minimums. Problems it catches are flagged as you draft — look over the schedule before you post it.

No employee accounts. Ever.

Only the manager signs in. Staff get their schedules the way they already live — text, email, print, or a calendar file that drops shifts straight into their phones. Nothing to install. Nobody to onboard.

Fair — and you can see it

Sought-after shifts rotate by seniority, by turns, or by an open lottery. A running ledger tracks who you've given what, so the same people never quietly draw the short straw — and you can see it at a glance.

Built for workplaces with real rules

Ratios between roles — a licensed person to every so many assistants, a supervisor on every shift — license and certification expiry tracking, posts that must never sit empty with named meal-break relief, and two people who must not work together kept apart — by day, by hours or by post — with nobody but you ever seeing the rule. The depth enterprise scheduling suites keep for enterprise contracts — at a flat small-business price.

Your data stays yours

Schedules live in your private account and follow you between devices. We run no ads and no ad trackers, we don't sell your data, and everything exports as a spreadsheet, a PDF or a calendar file whenever you want it. Your staff’s names, numbers and pay rates are never sold, rented, or handed to anyone for their own purposes; that one is written into the terms, not just promised here.

The break-rules engine

Break rules aren't a report you run. They're built into every draft.

Meal and rest break rules differ by state. Weeksolved carries the break rules for the states it supports and applies them while drafting: how long a shift runs before a meal is owed, the latest the rule allows it to start, how many paid rest breaks the hours earn, and when a waiver is allowed.
