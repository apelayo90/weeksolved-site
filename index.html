Why managers switch

The schedule does what you'd do — just in seconds

Most scheduling apps hand you an empty grid and wish you luck. Weeksolved asks what your week has to look like, then builds it — and shows its work.

It never invents a shift

Only the shifts you've defined ever get scheduled. If coverage can't be met from that pool, the slot stays open and it names exactly which limit stopped each person — then offers the best ways out, clearly labeled, least disruptive first.

It knows your break law

The break-law engine

Compliance isn't a report you run. It's how the schedule gets built.

Meal and rest break rules differ by state, and the penalties are real — in California, one missed or late meal period owes an hour of premium pay, per person, per day. Weeksolved carries each state's rules and applies them while drafting: how long a shift runs before a meal is owed, the latest legal minute it can start, how many paid rest breaks the hours earn, and when a waiver is allowed.
